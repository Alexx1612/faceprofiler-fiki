What this website collects, and why

Short and plain. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Nau-ino is a student project at the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest. The team decides what this website collects. Contact: alexandru.mirea1612@stud.acs.upb.ro.

This page is about the website only. The Nau-ino camera software keeps its data inside each school and has its own rules.

We never sell or share your data with advertisers. We do not collect anything from the camera demos on this site: the photos and videos are fictional people or stock footage.

Your choice on the cookie bar

Accept lets us keep an anonymous id in your browser between visits, look up your approximate location (country and region, never the IP address or city) and record your visit with typed fields hidden. Decline keeps the visit anonymous for this tab only, with no location lookup and no recording. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted at all. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Where it is stored

  • Form answers, sign-ups and visit statistics: a Google Sheet and Google Drive folder in the team's Google account (Google acts as our processor).
  • Google sign-in on the pilot form: Google confirms your name and e-mail; we receive nothing else from your Google account.
  • Web statistics may also go to PostHog, hosted in the European Union.
  • The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, withdraw your consent, or object, by writing to alexandru.mirea1612@stud.acs.upb.ro. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro).
