Faster than a guard?

Spot the one new face. You against the machine.

Round 1/4 · 3 known + 1 new
● REC · CAM 02

How to play · about 3 minutes

  1. 1Remember 3 faces. Then find the one new person in the camera feed and tap them.
  2. 2The same with 14 faces.
  3. 3A busy scene: tap the person who is about to hurt someone. Frowns and hoods are not clues.
  4. 4Control room: 4 cameras, 30 people on the list. Find the intruder and the person about to cause harm, and lock in both.

You against Nau-ino: at the end you see your time next to the system's.

System time estimated from Nau-ino measurements on a laptop CPU: ≈43 ms per face, 5 frames/s, 3 frames to confirm.

Two problems, one set of cameras.

What teachers and a deputy headmistress told us at two schools.

School hours

Day

The risk is between people you know: a fight in a corridor, a child walking out alone.

Nau-ino follows known faces from camera to camera and shows staff where to look.

After hours

Night

The risk is anyone at all, and the guard is on a round, not at the screen.

Every face is checked. A stranger is flagged the moment they appear, with the cameras that saw them.

Faces stay in the building.

  • Runs at the schoolOn a computer in the building. Video and faces are not sent to a cloud.
  • Only enrolled people are namedEveryone else is Unknown_1, Unknown_2. Nobody is looked up anywhere else.
  • No emotion analysisIt stays off in schools: the EU AI Act bans it there.
