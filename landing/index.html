School hours

Day

The risk is between people you know: a fight in a corridor, a child walking out alone.

Nau-ino follows known faces from camera to camera and shows staff where to look.

After hours

Night

The risk is anyone at all, and the guard is on a round, not at the screen.

Every face is checked. A stranger is flagged the moment they appear, with the cameras that saw them.

Faces stay in the building.

  • Runs at the schoolOn a computer in the building. Video and faces are not sent to a cloud.
  • Only enrolled people are namedEveryone else is Unknown_1, Unknown_2. Nobody is looked up anywhere else.
  • No emotion analysisIt stays off in schools: the EU AI Act bans it there.
