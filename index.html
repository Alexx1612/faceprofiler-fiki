Nau-ino

Security, access control and analytics for any site, on the CCTV cameras it already has, at a low energy cost.

From kindergartens and offices to data centres and military bases: Nau-ino checks who enters with face recognition on existing cameras, confirms the face is real by its pulse, and checks visitors in and out with keycards or single-use passes. Each organisation's data stays in its own isolated container, and a local LLM turns events and notes into reports anyone can read. The same cameras give footfall analytics and forecasts, and neuromorphic models keep the energy cost low.

Problem · Solution · Market

Hours of video that nobody watches

Sites record around the clock, yet the footage is used only after something has gone wrong.

The problem

Cameras record, nobody reacts

  • Footage nobody watches. Malls, shops, offices and care homes record 24/7. A guard watching a wall of screens misses most of what happens.
  • Searching is manual. Finding one person in a day of recordings means scrubbing through hours of video by hand.
  • Alerts come too late. After-hours break-ins, thefts, falls and fights are often found the next morning, not when they happen.
  • The data goes unused. The same video could show how people move through a space and where they stop. Nobody turns it into a report.

Solution & value proposition

A security layer on existing cameras

Nau-ino recognises enrolled people, keeps strangers anonymous and alerts staff only when a human is needed. Every event is saved with evidence and turned into a readable report.

Know who is on site, find anyone in minutes instead of hours, without new cameras and without sending a single face to the cloud.

Business fundamentals

Who buys, why, and how it pays

Retail

Shopping centres, chain stores, showrooms

Pain: Theft and no idea which displays work.

Gets: Unknown-person alerts after hours, plus footfall, dwell time and attention heatmaps per display.

Signs: Store operations or security manager

Restricted sites

Offices, labs, data centres, military bases

Pain: Badges get shared; tailgating goes unnoticed.

Gets: Everyone who enters must be known. An unauthorised face is flagged the moment it appears.

Signs: Facility or security manager

Care & schools

Kindergartens, schools, care homes

Pain: Staff cannot watch every room; falls are found late.

Gets: Alerts for an unknown adult, a fall or a fight. Emotion analysis stays off here: the EU AI Act bans it in schools and workplaces.

Signs: Director or administrator

Integrators

Companies that install and maintain CCTV

Pain: Customers want AI but replacing cameras is expensive.

Gets: A licence they install on existing systems and resell with their maintenance contract.

Signs: Integrator owner (channel partner)
